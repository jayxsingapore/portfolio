Part IV · F1 Trophy

Power, poise
and elegance.

Driven by passion, my love for racing: the Formula 1 Singapore Airlines F1 Trophy, based on the Singapore Airlines bird symbol.

The bird symbol is brought to life in a three-dimensional sculptural form. The three gold stripes echo the three wings of the symbol and are highlighted with gold plating.

The rings on the base signify the number of Grands Prix held in Singapore, with a new one added each year. Each trophy is gold plated and milled from solid aircraft-grade aluminium.

Scope

3D, concepts, brand, UX, digital and integrated campaigns.

Part V · Bearie

Bringing joy to
young travellers.

Bringing joy to young travellers through engaging character design and interactive experiences.

The objective was to create kids’ meal bags to replace the existing plastic ones, and to encourage children to take part in the activities on the bags, like join-the-dots and finding the hidden Bearie. New bags are created each year, with different themes and illustrations. Young local talent was commissioned for all the illustrations and the plush prototype.

Bearie is a lovable plush character for Singapore Airlines’ youngest passengers, alongside a suite of interactive travel bags and companion assets. The project combined 3D character creation, playful storytelling and experiential design to enhance the in-flight journey, creating a memorable, brand-aligned experience that engages children while supporting the airline’s family-focused positioning.

Scope

3D, concept development, character creation, brand and integrated campaigns.

The impact

Enhanced engagement for families, strengthened brand perception, and a differentiated, emotionally resonant travel experience.
