Led creative exploration of a future-facing visual identity for Singapore Airlines, evolving brand language across identity, product, and customer experience.

This included reinterpreting core assets such as the batik motif, extending the brand across new product concepts, and defining a cohesive, modern design system that honours the airline's iconic heritage while positioning it confidently for the next era of travel.

The work also included the F1 Trophy Design, which went on to receive Gold at the A' Design Awards and Silver at the International Design Awards.
