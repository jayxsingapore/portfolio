The story

A story
worth telling.

Some stories are simply too extraordinary to be forgotten.

During WWII, a DC-3 Dakota carrying a consignment of diamonds was attacked by Japanese Zero fighters over Northern Australia in 1942. Forced to land, the aircraft and its precious cargo became the centre of a mystery that has endured for decades.

Among the crew was the grandfather of Peccavi Wines owner, Jeremy. It was this remarkable personal connection, combined with the mystery of the missing diamonds, that inspired the Dakota concept: an opportunity to transform an extraordinary piece of history into an immersive wine experience.

Vision

I wanted to create something that would spark curiosity, generate excitement and encourage people to explore the story.

The starting point was to understand the history, uncover its characters and events, and explore how these could be translated into a distinctive, multi-layered wine experience.

The mark

The logo is inspired by the Dakota's propeller and the diamond cut pattern, with a bold, stylish typographic treatment. The horizontal line suggests a runway or landing strip.

Part I · Limited Edition Wooden Case

A premium
experience.

Conceived as a collectible presentation, the bespoke wooden case was designed to make the Dakota story tangible.

Every element was carefully considered, bringing together a bottle of Peccavi Red, a commemorative crystal glass, an illustrated map wrap and nine collectible coasters.

Natural materials, understated detailing and a restrained visual language give the presentation a sense of authenticity and heritage.

The map wrap reveals the geographical context of the Dakota's final journey, while the bottle's neck label retains the brand identity once the wrap is removed.

More than a presentation box, it is the beginning of a story waiting to be discovered.

The interactive experience

An experience that
invites discovery.

The story continues inside the box.

Integrated into the hinged lid is a bespoke radio box, designed to play nine recorded transmissions, each corresponding to one of nine individually illustrated coasters.

The coasters were conceived as collectible pieces, depicting characters, aircraft and key moments from the Dakota story. Each was designed to be scanned using augmented reality, unlocking another episode and revealing additional layers of the story.

The radio adds another dimension, with nine recorded transmissions providing audio insight and commentary. Together, the radio and AR coasters create a richer, multi-layered storytelling experience.

I developed the bespoke radio fascia and helped bring the physical prototype to life, working collaboratively with the team on its construction, playback electronics and recorded content.

Part II · Everyday Wine Bottle

Five bottles.
Five episodes.
One mystery.

The Dakota story was designed to reach beyond the Limited Edition presentation.

A collection of five everyday wine bottles brought the concept into a more accessible retail format. Each featured a distinctive illustration, revealing a different character, moment or perspective from the Dakota mystery.

Each bottle was designed to offer its own augmented reality experience. Scanning the label would unlock an episode of the story, combining visual and audio content to bring the aircraft, its crew and the events surrounding its disappearance to life.

While the Limited Edition offered nine episodes through its interactive coasters and radio, the everyday collection provided five new ways to discover the story.
