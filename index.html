About

I'm Sanjay, a Strategic Brand & Identity Systems Designer based in Singapore with over 15 years building visual systems for corporate and institutional organisations, from global airlines to national trade unions.

My practice sits at the intersection of brand strategy and disciplined visual craft. I translate positioning into cohesive identity architectures: logo systems, typographic frameworks, colour structures, and the governance guidelines that make them hold at scale.

Calm, structured, and rationale-led; I align senior stakeholders, direct cross-functional teams, and ensure brand coherence across campaigns, publications, digital platforms, and spatial environments.

"The best brands don't just look good; they make the right argument, every single time, without saying a word."
