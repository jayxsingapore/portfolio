The opportunity

A new perspective on
premium wellbeing.

Create a distinctive premium sub-brand for Blackmores, targeting high-net-worth individuals, predominantly male C-suite executives and frequent international travellers.

The audience

Time-poor, globally mobile executives with demanding lifestyles, who value quality, convenience and discretion but often overlook their own nutritional wellbeing.

The creative direction

Developed around the Blackmores name, ‘black’ was selected. Black-on-black packaging, contrasting matt and gloss finishes and considered metallic detailing.

The ambition was a premium supplement proposition that reflected the expectations and lifestyle of a discerning executive audience.
